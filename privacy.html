Pocketune

Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026. Publisher: Muzammil Peer, Singapore.

Pocketune is a music player for iPhone. It has no accounts, adverts, tracking or developer analytics, and it does not send your listening to any server run by the developer.

Information stored on your device

Pocketune stores your player preferences, saved player combinations, favourite song identifiers and up to 50 recent song identifiers in local app preferences. Your device backup may include those preferences according to your Apple settings.

Apple Music

Connecting Apple Music requires your permission. The app uses Apple's MusicKit framework to browse eligible music, check subscription capabilities and play the songs you choose. Apple processes service requests, artwork and playback according to its own terms and privacy policies. You can use the included demonstration music without granting music library access.

Companion widget

The app shares the current song title, artist, artwork address, playback state, progress and selected screen colours with its own widget through a local Apple App Group container on your device. The widget may fetch artwork over HTTPS from Apple's artwork servers. It never receives music authorisation tokens or payment details.

Purchases

Apple processes Collector purchases and restorations through the App Store. Pocketune checks Apple's verified purchase record to decide whether Collector features are available. The developer never receives your payment details.

Developer collection and tracking

Pocketune does not collect personal data. It contains no developer-hosted account system, advertising SDK, tracking SDK or analytics service. Apple may process device diagnostics according to your settings.

Your choices

Switch to the demo music in Settings to stop using the Apple Music integration. Revoke music access in your iPhone's Settings app. Clear recent listening in Pocketune's Settings, remove favourites individually and delete saved player combinations in the collection. Deleting the app removes its local data from that iPhone; backups follow your backup settings.

Children

Pocketune does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date. Questions: muzammil.peer@gmail.com.
