Getting around

Turn the wheel to move through menus. Press the centre to choose. Tap MENU to go back one level, or hold MENU to return to the main menu from anywhere. Hold the centre on a song to add it to Up Next.

Apple Music

Go to Menu, then Settings, then Connect Apple Music. Streaming the Apple Music catalogue needs your own Apple Music subscription. Without one, you can still play eligible music you have bought or synced. If Pocketune cannot see your music, check that music access is allowed for Pocketune in your iPhone's Settings app.

Collector

Silver is free with unlimited listening. Collector is a one-time purchase that unlocks six additional player designs, customisation and saved combinations. If you change iPhone, choose Restore Purchases in Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Lock Screen and StandBy

Add the Pocketune Companion widget from your Lock Screen, Home Screen or StandBy widget picker. It shows the current song with simple playback buttons. iOS controls where widgets appear and how often they refresh.

Reporting a problem

Email the address above with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help.
